## Third-party work

iQuantize builds on the work of these projects:

- [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp): GGUF conversion, quantization, evaluation and inference
- [MLX](https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx) and [mlx-lm](https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-lm): quantization and inference on Apple Silicon
- [ONNX Runtime GenAI](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime-genai): ONNX model building and inference
- [Hugging Face Hub](https://github.com/huggingface/huggingface_hub): model downloads and publishing
- [uv](https://github.com/astral-sh/uv): Python and package management
- [Tauri](https://tauri.app): the desktop application framework
- [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter) (font) and [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (icons), bundled under their own licences
